Recognition

Competition result, reviews and talks

RF-TraceGuard was first shown in public at a hackathon in April 2025. Since then independent specialists have reviewed it, and the approach has been presented at engineering conferences.

01 / Competition

Winner, Best Innovative Solution for Industry, 2025

The All-Russian hackathon “Best Innovations: Practice”, track “Best IT Solutions for Business”, took place online from 15 to 20 April 2025. The Best Talents association organized it with the support of the St. Petersburg Chamber of Commerce and Industry. The organizers received more than 1,650 applications, 18 projects reached the final, and winners were named in three categories. RF-TraceGuard won the category “Best Innovative Solution for Industry”.

9.28 / 10

final score of RF-TraceGuard, the highest among all 18 finalists

8.14

final score of the project ranked second

7.76

mean final score of the finalists

For technical complexity and quality of implementation, the criterion with a 30% weight, the jury gave 9.5 out of 10. The jury comments singled out the description of the whole measurement path, with its equipment, channels, operating ranges, calibration and reference data and measurement uncertainty, and the split of the solution into modules with clear tasks, from the description of the station to result integrity, drift monitoring and the search for the cause of a deviation. The jury also noted the practical value of seeing station problems before they reach product results.

02 / Independent reviews

Independent reviews of RF-TraceGuard

Kamaldeen Folarin Jimoh and Valery V. Mikhailov studied the technical material and gave written opinions. They work independently of the author, in their own organizations and fields. The full letters are available on request.

Kamaldeen Folarin JimohRF engineer, Independent Researcher (RF & DSP) at Double Decade Engineering. Formerly RF System Integration Engineer at Nokia and RF Product Engineer at Ericsson.
In simple terms, the golden unit tells you that something is wrong, while RF-TraceGuard tells you where the problem is.
RF-TraceGuard is an original and practically valuable contribution to RF production testing.Expert letter, 6 October 2026

He studied the technical brief, put technical questions to the author and sent written comments. His letter singles out the verdict for each individual result, the drift monitor working on the periodic reference measurements of the station components, and above all the localization of the cause. He also proposed directions for a next version: test lines with active components such as RF power amplifiers, and stations built around a vector network analyzer, with temperature taken into account.

Valery V. MikhailovCandidate of Technical Sciences, Associate Professor. Independent researcher, Republic of Belarus.
The chain becomes the object of monitoring, and the stream of test results becomes the carrier of information about its condition.
A method that survives such a transfer rests on a property common to a broad class of measurement problems.Expert opinion, 12 October 2026

His own work covers force and torque measuring devices, gear test rigs and the verification of control systems for unmanned vehicles. He and the author spoke one after the other in the same session of GCNSI-2026. He plans to use RF-TraceGuard on an experimental bench between a physical object and its digital twin, to tell degradation of the object from loss of validity in the measurement channel.
