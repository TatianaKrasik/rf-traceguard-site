About

Dr. Tatiana Krasik

RF and microwave measurement engineer and independent researcher based in Orlando, Florida. Developer of RF-TraceGuard.

Her work joins formal metrology and production test: verification of measurements, statements of uncertainty and the fate of a result when part of the measuring chain changes between calibrations. She is an IEEE Senior Member and holds a PhD (Candidate of Technical Sciences, 2012).

05 / Origin

The origin of RF-TraceGuard

The idea came from years in RF production test. There a drifting cable or a worn connector looks exactly like a faulty product, and engineers often find the cause by swapping parts one at a time. Her metrology work added another habit: every measurement channel has its own error sources, limits and checks. RF-TraceGuard applies that metrological discipline to a working production line, between calibrations.

RF-TraceGuard was developed independently, as her own project. It was first shown at a hackathon in April 2025 and has since been applied in production, in a microwave laboratory and at a university.
